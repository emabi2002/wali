About WALI

A practical Papua New Guinea service company

WALI brings air-conditioning, refrigeration, electrical and building-control knowledge together under one accountable team.

Company overview

Built to support the systems clients depend on

WALI Airconditioning and Electricals Limited is based in Port Moresby and provides installation, maintenance, fault diagnosis and system-improvement services for residential, commercial, industrial and institutional clients.

Our technical capability covers HVAC systems, electrical installations, refrigeration controls, motor controls and Building Management Systems. This combined understanding helps us investigate complete systems instead of treating each component in isolation.

Vision

A trusted PNG technical service provider

To become a trusted Papua New Guinea provider of air-conditioning, refrigeration, electrical and intelligent building services.

Mission

Dependable work and clear support

To deliver installation, maintenance and technical support through competent workmanship, safe work practices, clear reporting and responsive service.

Purpose

Reliable and safe facilities

To help clients operate reliable, energy-conscious and safe facilities through practical technical services.
